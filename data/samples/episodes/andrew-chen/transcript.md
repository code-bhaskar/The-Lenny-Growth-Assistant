Andrew Chen: A good growth loop starts with user value. If the loop depends on spammy invitations or low-intent traffic, it may boost signups but it rarely compounds.

Andrew Chen: Retention is the scoreboard for growth quality. Teams often celebrate acquisition metrics before they have earned habitual usage. The better move is to identify the core action, measure how quickly new users reach it, and improve that path.

Andrew Chen: Channels saturate. That is why the best teams keep building product-native distribution rather than treating growth like a paid-media spreadsheet.
