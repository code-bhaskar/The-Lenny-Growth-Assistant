Lenny Rachitsky: Product-market fit is rarely one moment. It often appears first in a narrow segment that loves the product more intensely than everyone else.

Lenny Rachitsky: Instead of asking whether the whole market wants your product, find the users who would be very disappointed if it disappeared. Their language helps you understand the job to be done.

Lenny Rachitsky: Follow-up questions matter. When a user says they love the product, ask what they would do instead if your product no longer existed. The substitute reveals the real competition.
