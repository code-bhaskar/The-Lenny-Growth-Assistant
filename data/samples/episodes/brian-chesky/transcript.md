Lenny: What changed the most at Airbnb when growth became more disciplined?

Brian Chesky: The biggest shift was moving from generic roadmaps to a small number of high-conviction bets. We learned that clarity beats feature sprawl. If a team cannot explain the customer problem in a sentence, they are probably not ready to ship.

Brian Chesky: Product taste is not decoration. It is the ability to notice what customers feel but do not always articulate. Great product leaders review the details, not because they want control for its own sake, but because the details compound into trust.

Brian Chesky: We also tightened the feedback loop. Designers, PMs, and engineers looked at customer reactions every week, not every quarter.
